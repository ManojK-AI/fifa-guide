# EA SPORTS FC 26: CPU AI, difficulty, gameplay presets and patches (research for coaching page)

Compiled 2026-10-06. Every source is about FC 26 unless the line is tagged [FC 25], [FC 27] or [FIFA 23].

**How the evidence was gathered (read first)**
- Quotes come from WebFetch. Its extractor caps each quote at about 125 characters, so I requested long bullets in pieces. "…" means I left words out. Anything the tool paraphrased is shown without quotation marks or left out.
- EA forum posts only show relative ages ("9 months ago"). I report them as they read on 2026-10-06.
- The WebSearch budget for this run (200 calls) ran out near the end. Remaining gaps are marked "not found".
- I could not fetch fifplay.com/fc-26-difficulty-levels/ because the permission prompt went unanswered, so I don't know if that page exists.
- Confidence levels: **high** means EA's own page, or an outlet reproducing EA's notes. **med** means a reputable outlet or fan site, or several forum posts that agree. **low** means one forum anecdote or a low-tier site.

## 1. Squad Battles in FC 26 Ultimate Team

**Does it exist? Yes.**
- It is a solo mode against AI squads. — "Squad Battles is a single-player mode in Ultimate Team where you face off against AI-controlled squads." / "Squad Battles is available in FC 26 > Ultimate Team > Play > Play vs AI > Squad Battles" — [FIFPlay FC 26 Squad Battles, 2025-08-02](https://www.fifplay.com/fc-26-squad-battles/) — high
- EA's FUT Deep Dive puts Squad Battles in the new "Play vs. CPU" menu, and nowhere else. — [EA Pitch Notes FUT Deep Dive, 2025-08-05](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-fut-deep-dive) — high

**Which difficulties? Beginner to Ultimate, and Ultimate is included.** I found no EA page that lists them.
- The points table includes Ultimate. — "This multiplier reflects the challenge of the match. It varies depending on the selected difficulty level and whether you win or lose". Win/loss multipliers: Legendary 2.1/0.7, Ultimate 2.6/0.7. Goal bonus: Legendary 140 per goal (max 700), Ultimate 160 per goal (max 800). — [FIFAUTeam FC 26 Squad Battle Points, pub 2025-08-17, mod 2025-10-20](https://fifauteam.com/fc-26-squad-battle-points/) — med
- Players refer to Ultimate in Squad Battles. — "…this is still challenging enough to make me afraid to lose points trying ultimate difficulty (which imo is the way it should be)" (arsenalfanrichi) — [EA Forums "Squad Battle AI Patch", 10 months ago](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-26-ultimate-team-en/squad-battle-ai-patch/12963224) — med
- Players refer to Ultimate in Squad Battles. — "in Ultimate Team's Squad Battles, the CPU on Ultimate difficulty plays with incredible intensity." (CocoMachiavelli) — [EA Forums "Career Difficulty Level doesn't work…", 11 months ago](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-26-feedback-en/career-difficulty-level-doesn%E2%80%99t-work-all-are-very-easy-/12613542/replies/12762425) — med
- Easy, Medium and Hard are opponent-squad pools, not CPU difficulty levels. — "One squad from each difficulty group: Easy, Medium, and Hard." / "A fourth squad randomly selected from either the Easy or Medium pool (50/50 chance)." — [FIFPlay FC 26 Squad Battles, 2025-08-02](https://www.fifplay.com/fc-26-squad-battles/) — med

- More matches per week. — "In response to players asking for more depth in Squad Battles in FC 25, players can now play up to 18 matches per week (increased from 14) that count towards their weekly point total." — [EA Pitch Notes Launch Update, 2025-09-12](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-launch-update) — high
- Squad Battles runs on Competitive tuning, and the CPU-controlled player has no fatigue. — "CPU-controlled players in Competitive tuning are also not affected by fatigue." / "An example is when playing Squad Battles." — [EA Pitch Notes Gameplay Deep Dive, 2025-07-22](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive) — high
- v1.1.3 changed CPU decision-making in offline modes, which includes Squad Battles. — "Driven by your Career Mode feedback, we're updating CPU AI decision making with the intent of making it challenging and engaging to play against, especially on higher difficulties." - EA acknowledged the Squad Battles CPU was hogging the ball. — "We've seen your feedback that the CPU AI in Squad Battles can hold the ball for too long and plays an overly possession based game at times." / "We're planning to make the Squad Battles CPU AI further focus on moving the ball forward and creating chances quicker." / "…will be targeting an early 2026 update." — [EA Pitch Notes November Feedback Update, 2025-11-25](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-feedback-update-november) — high
- EA restated the fix and said why. — "We've heard your feedback about the Squad Battles CPU AI becoming too difficult and frustrating to play against, especially on higher difficulties, due to its focus on maintaining possession. With the next update, the Squad Battles CPU AI will now focus more on moving the ball up the pitch while creating chances quicker." (EA_Vendcera, Community Manager) — [EA Forums December Feedback Update, 9 months ago](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-26-feedback-en/fc-26-december-feedback-update/13057507) — high
- v1.4.0 made the Squad Battles CPU more direct. Notes dated 2026-01-05; MP1st reports release on 2026-01-06. — "CPU AI behavior in Squad Battles has been adjusted." / "We heard your feedback that the recent tuning made the sequences feel overly passive and there were long periods of low-pressure possession from the CPU AI." / "The CPU will now take a more proactive and attacking approach to create a smoother Squad Battles experience." / "The CPU AI will not focus on maintaining possession as much as before." — [EA Pitch Notes TU v1.4.0, 2026-01-05](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-title-update-1-4-0) — high

## 3. Competitive vs Authentic gameplay

**What the split is**
- "In FC 26, we've separated gameplay into two presets: Competitive and Authentic." — [EA Gameplay Deep Dive, 2025-07-22](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive) — high
- "Competitive Gameplay is tuned for consistent responsiveness, mainly focused on online modes like Ultimate Team and Clubs." / "Authentic Gameplay is tuned for higher realism and true-to-life match tempo/speed in offline modes, like Career Mode." — [EA Gameplay Responsiveness Update, 2025-07-16](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-responsiveness-update) — high
- "Competitive Gameplay is a new tuning set made specifically for FUT, built around three core ideas: responsiveness, more consistency, and higher player control." — [EA FUT Deep Dive, 2025-08-05](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-fut-deep-dive) — high

**What Competitive changes.** EA lists 14 bullets, quoted exactly from the [Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive). Confidence: high.
- Goalkeepers: "All GK deflections try to get away from danger, reducing rebound goals"
- Defending: "Reduced tackles/blocks/interceptions bouncing back to your opponent" / "Less AI defending, resulting in looser defensive lines" / "Fewer auto tackles and auto blocks"
- Fatigue: "Redesigned Fatigue feature"
- Responsiveness: "More responsive and smoother transitions in and out of specific animations"
- Passing: "Increased passing speed and responsiveness". Elsewhere in the article: "Note that in Competitive Gameplay, first-time pass accuracy is reduced, despite being more responsive"
- Speed: "Tighter control and more speed while dribbling, and skill moves are faster"
- Crosses and headers: "Increased variability on crosses, to favour player skill over repetitive wide play" / "Increased heading error relative to Authentic"
- Corners: "Increase variability in Corner Kicks outcomes, with fewer attacking players involved by default"
- Other: "Less authentic visuals when avoiding a ball, to resolve these situations more effectively" / "Players get up quickly after being tackled to the ground" / "Players show no reaction animations when hit by a fast incoming ball"
- From the FUT Deep Dive: "Adjusted player stamina, so controlled players stay agile longer, encouraging active defending and smart switching" / "More responsive one-touch passing and shooting, now triggerable as little as one frame before receiving the ball" — high
- Fatigue detail: "Short-term and long-term fatigue only affects AI-controlled players" / "For AI players, they will have a reduction in Sprint Speed and Acceleration when fatigued" / "CPU-controlled players in Competitive tuning are also not affected by fatigue." / "For Authentic Gameplay, fatigue works as before" — [Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive) — high

**What Authentic changes.** Quoted exactly from the Deep Dive's "Authenticity Features Toggle" list. Confidence: high.
- Speed: "Lower speeds for dribbling and skill moves"
- Goalkeepers: "More physics-based GK rebounds, blocks, and bounces"
- Defending: "More realistic tackle accuracy" / "Greater AI defending enabled, with tighter defensive lines and defenders reacting realistically when a ball is nearby" / "More realistic and tighter positioning in midfield"
- Headers, crosses and corners: "More realistic header passing and shooting accuracy" / "Corner kicks and Crossing tuned to match real-world success rates"
- Other: "Authentic Sliders" / "Weather Effects, including Wind, Rain, and Snow" / "Players avoiding a ball might occasionally be hit by it instead" / "More realistic animation transitions" / "Human-like reactions and get up animations"
- New sliders: Header Shot Error, Header Pass Error, Intercept Error, Ball Deflection Error and Tackle Assistance.
- CPU team styles, listed under Authentic. EA does not say whether this also applies to Competitive.
  - "We've made improvements to the playing styles of CPU Teams to make them more unique, and to stand out in both attack and defence."
  - "CPU opponents will now make smarter and more varied decisions when it comes to passing, crossing, dribbling, and other gameplay areas."
  - "For example, some teams prefer to cross into the box more often, while others utilize slow buildup or fast tiki-taka attacking plays."
  - "The CPU will know better when and how to defend, press the player, stay back, or use double contain."
  - "For example, specific teams will try to overwhelm you by pressing with multiple players at once, while others will just sit back and then press you aggressively when you get close."
  - Confidence: high

### Gameplay Deep Dive, 2025-07-22 ([link](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive)). All lines high confidence.

**CPU/AI**
- "Call for Pass Improvements - will be more intelligent when considering pass requests and will prefer a better option when multiple requests come in at the same time."
- "This change is important for Rush and Clubs, since it means a CPU Goalkeeper will choose better pass options when players spam the pass request."
- "Powering Up - the CPU now powers up shots and passes the same way human players do, allowing for a more authentic experience in certain plays and contexts."
- Also relevant: the fatigue lines and Team's Playing Styles in Section 3.

**Goalkeepers**
- "Deflection Control - added a new level of control for save deflections to significantly reduce the amount of deflections going back to opponents."
- "Most deflections will now go out of bounds, toward teammates, or to open space away from opponents."
- "Goalkeeper Senses - refined goalkeeper's ability to read shot trajectories to better reflect real-world save efforts and shot detection."
- "Technical Improvements - improved base systems for goalkeeper handling, more consistent shot-stopping, and more reasonable decision making in one-on-one situations."
- "This new system uses machine learning to help AI-controlled goalkeepers make more realistic decisions about where to position themselves in the box."
- "By analyzing countless scenarios, the AI can now better cut down angles and react to different types of attacks while adjusting their position more intelligently."
- "It's important to note that RL Positioning is only active in certain situations, and is only used to position the Goalkeeper."
- "The system is not active when making a save or performing a catch."
- Nothing on near-post shots or line of sight.

**Defending**
- "We know how it can feel to time a tackle perfectly only for the ball to go right back to the opponent."
- "Tackle Outcomes - improved logic when Stand or Slide tackling the ball to a teammate."
- "Shot Blocks - added a new deflection control system to make blocked shots put the ball away from danger."
- "Interceptions - added a new deflection control system to try to keep possession or move the ball to a teammate when making an interception."
- "Tackled Opponents - opponents will feel the effect of a tackle more severely when dispossessed of the ball…"
- "Tackle Spam - improved logic for spamming tackle, to make the delay after a missed tackle more consistent."
- "Tackle vs Block - when pressing Stand Tackle near the dribbler about to take a shot, the defender will be more likely to try to tackle rather than block."
- "Tackles From Behind - reduced the effectiveness of tackling from behind the dribbler."
- Tackle Personality by stand-tackle rating:
  - "Players with a low stand tackle attribute (below 71) can only perform basic stand tackle animations."
  - "Players with a medium stand tackle attribute (71-84) can perform most stand tackle animations."
  - "Players with a high stand tackle attribute (85+) can perform all stand tackle animations."
- Jockey (Movement list): "improved Jockey movement to be more agile and responsive"
- Contain and teammate contain: not mentioned.

**Passing**
- "Passing Angles - rebalanced animation restrictions to allow passing to cover a broader range of angles."
- "Quick Passes - Optimized short-distance, low-power passes to prefer faster, more responsive animations with minimal wind-up."
- "Pass Receiver - improved pass receiver selection, especially for Ground and Through passes."
- "Crosses - adjusted Crosses to lead more in front of the target, along with increased accuracy when in easy contexts."
- "Ground Passes - regular Ground passes will now have a straighter trajectory with less contextual curve."
- "Lob Passes - Lob passes will have more driven trajectories when the path is unobstructed."
- "Clearances - adjusted Clearance logic to make them more responsive."
- Through passes:
  - "We've introduced the concept of Passing Lanes for semi-assisted through passes."
  - "Aiming at a specific passing lane (the gap between two opponents) ensures the best target selection for the intended receiver within that lane, respecting your aim and intention."
  - "Through passes will also now lead receivers more effectively."
- Lobbed through passes: no specific line.
- Headers: see the Competitive and Authentic bullets in Section 3, and the PlayStyles below.

**Shooting**
- "Close-range Shots - shots near the net will use more reactive animations with minimal wind-up."
- "Shooting Angles - rebalanced animation restrictions to enable shooting to cover a broader range of angles."
- Low driven:
  - "In FC 26, Low Driven shots will be performed by double-tapping the shot button."
  - "Low Driven is now a global* shot modifier instead of being contextual based on your shot power."
- Timed finishing:
  - "In general, we felt Timed Finishing didn't add meaningful skill depth and proved difficult to balance."
  - "It disproportionately rewarded long-range shots and felt inconsistent in high-pressure moments."
  - "Removing it allows us to balance shooting to produce more consistent outcomes."
- Finesse, power, chip and first-time shots: no specific line beyond the Low Driven variants.

**Dribbling and shielding**
- "In FC 26, dribbling has received a significant amount of changes to improve responsiveness and fluidity."
- "Dribble Touch Intervals - re-tuned how close the player keeps the ball to themselves when dribbling."
- Jog Dribble: "increased speed of jog dribbling alongside improved logic for ball direction"
- Agile Dribbling Removal: "removed the Agile Dribbling mechanic due to its low usage"
- "Shield Trapping - our objective was to give players a new tool when trapping the ball."
- "Dribble shielding has been fully reworked to make it more consistent and better at respecting your input."
- Dribbling Shoulder Challenges: "New type of Shielding action that allows you to better protect the ball while dribbling."
- "Contested Box Outs. Stronger players are able to keep opponents away when fighting for an incoming air ball."
- "First Touch Responsiveness - significantly improved responsiveness of first touch ball control."

**Positioning and defensive line**
- "Increased the number of attacking runs by players, to lower reliance on triggered runs."
- "Attacking runs more consistently run into open space."
- Offside Runs: "improved attacker's ability to stay onside, and the speed in which they come back when offside."
- Player Marking: "adjusted positioning of defenders to now man mark opponents when close and inside the box."
- Defensive Line Height: "the defensive line will not automatically push up as far when trying to keep players offside."
- "Offside Calls - remade the offside logic to improve the accuracy of calls."
- "Breakaway Fouls - improved our breakaway logic to better detect fouls involving the last player back."

**Pace and AcceleRATE**
- "Our goal with FC 26 is to make all players more agile and able to accelerate more explosively in any direction."
- "Explosive Sprint - increased player agility and explosiveness in every direction."
- Attacker Vs Defender Speed: "Improved the speed differentiation between Attackers versus Defenders to achieve an improved balance between the two."
- EA describes it as "going back to 3 Acceleration Types". The thresholds:
  - "Explosive: Agility minimum 65, (Agility - Strength) >= 10, Acceleration minimum 80, Height separated between male (max 182cm/~6'0") and female players (max 162cm/~5'4")."
  - "Lengthy: Strength minimum 65, (Strength - Agility) >= 4, Acceleration minimum 40, Height separated between male (min 183cm/~6'1") and female players (min 164cm/~5'5")."
  - "Controlled - any player that does not meet the other two requirements above."
  - v1.4.0 later changed the Lengthy height thresholds; see Section 5.

**Set pieces**
- "Corner Kicks - adjusted the positioning and run locations of attacking players to be less congested and reduced counter attacks."
- "Free Kicks - adjusted Free Kick trajectories after the removal of Timed Finishing."
- New corner roles: attacking corners get "Top of Box" and "Defensive Cover". Defending corners get "Threat Marker", "Post Watcher", "Near Post" and "Back Post".

**PlayStyles**
- "Rebalanced attribute system to make players with higher base attributes execute standard gameplay mechanics more reliably, even without the relevant PlayStyle."
- New: Enforcer, Aerial Fortress, Precision Header, Inventive and GameChanger.
- "Performs headers with increased accuracy and power." (Precision Header)
- "Relentless and Relentless+ will now also contribute to increasing the speed of short-term stamina regeneration."

### Gameplay Responsiveness Update, 2025-07-16 ([link](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-responsiveness-update)). High.
