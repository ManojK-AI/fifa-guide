[m43rrgjd38g.34] ## Pre-game card
[m43rrgjd38g.48] Read this once, then play. Legendary punishes rushed passes, not slow ones.
[m43rrgjd38g.124] **Mind**
[m43rrgjd38g.130] - Say your mantra: "cod dude." One slow breath in, one slow breath out.
[m43rrgjd38g.201] - Pick one focus from the gaps below for this match. Ignore the rest.
[m43rrgjd38g.270] - Target: concede one or fewer. That is the gap between your old 3-2 wins and dominating.
[m43rrgjd38g.359] **With the ball**
[m43rrgjd38g.374] 1. Walk, don't sprint. Sprint only into open grass.
[m43rrgjd38g.424] 2. Build from the back. Your keeper and center-backs are passers, not clearers.
[m43rrgjd38g.502] 3. Play diagonals. Straight passes run into lanes the AI already guards.
[m43rrgjd38g.573] 4. Bounce it: in, lay-off back, then forward. Whoever faces forward plays forward.
[m43rrgjd38g.654] 5. Drag them one way, pass the other. The free man finds the runner.
[m43rrgjd38g.721] 6. In the final third: get to the byline, then cut it back along the ground.
[m43rrgjd38g.796] **Without the ball**
[m43rrgjd38g.814] - Shape first, ball second. The back four never steps out to chase.
[m43rrgjd38g.881] - Control the midfielder who can cut the lane, not a center-back.
[m43rrgjd38g.946] - Their midfielder turns to face your goal: through-ball alert. Switch to the center-back near the runner and drop.
[m43rrgjd38g.1061] - Teammate contain in short bursts. Holding it drags your shape apart.
[m43rrgjd38g.1131] **Reset rules**
[m43rrgjd38g.1144] - Just conceded? Two minutes of safe passes before any risky ball.
[m43rrgjd38g.1210] - Lost it in their half? Block the middle first; don't sprint the nearest man at the ball.
[m43rrgjd38g.1300] - Leading late? Keep the ball. Passing backward is allowed.
[m43rrgjd38g.1359] ## Coach's verdict
[m43rrgjd38g.1375] Your principles are right; the gap is the first two-thirds of the pitch. Nearly every line in your notes is about the last 30 yards, and that's exactly where you said games break: center-back to midfield to attack. Even your old 3-2 and 3-1 wins leaked one or two goals a game, so dominating means conceding one or fewer.
[m43rrgjd38g.1697] **How your three problems connect**
[m43rrgjd38g.1730] - No build-up routine means forced passes through a crowded middle.
[m43rrgjd38g.1797] - Those passes get cut out in midfield while your full-backs are high. That's when Legendary plays the ball in behind you.
[m43rrgjd38g.1919] - When you do get forward, it's after a scramble, not a pattern. So you get lots of passing and nothing clear-cut.
[m43rrgjd38g.2033] - Fix the build-up and much of the other two goes with it.
[m43rrgjd38g.2091] **Keep these, they're correct**
[m43rrgjd38g.2120] - Patience, and walking instead of sprinting.
[m43rrgjd38g.2165] - The free man finds the runner (a third-man run).
[m43rrgjd38g.2215] - Drag defenders one way, pass the other.
[m43rrgjd38g.2256] - Diagonal runs, not straight lines.
[m43rrgjd38g.2292] - A back four that never dives in.
[m43rrgjd38g.2326] - First-time finishes in the box.
[m43rrgjd38g.2359] **Reframe one belief**
[m43rrgjd38g.2378] You wrote that the AI works on "ML probability." Whatever runs under the hood, the useful numbers are these: Legendary is set to 90% on attacking intelligence, defending intelligence, reaction speed and marking. Ultimate raises three of those to 100%.
[m43rrgjd38g.12758] In FC 26, EA hasn't published these numbers. What it has said: since update 1.4.0 (January 2026) the Squad Battles AI moves the ball forward quicker, and its defenders react slower while they turn.
[m43rrgjd38g.2630] So it shuts down the obvious option fast, which is why your deception rule works. When a pattern beats it, run it again until it stops working. Don't drop a working pattern just for variety.
[m43rrgjd38g.2821] **On the rust**
[m43rrgjd38g.2833] What fades after a layoff is timing: pass weight, through-ball release, when to switch. Principles don't fade; reps do. Rebuild timing before you judge yourself on results.
[m43rrgjd38g.3006] ## Gap 1: Getting the ball from defense to attack
[m43rrgjd38g.3053] This is your biggest gap, so fix it first. What's missing is a repeatable build-up routine.
[m43rrgjd38g.3145] **Why it breaks**
[m43rrgjd38g.3160] - Legendary presses your center-backs and parks bodies in the lanes straight ahead of them. A straight pass into midfield runs through ground it already guards.
[m43rrgjd38g.3320] - In 4-3-3 Attack with everything on Balanced, no midfielder is told to stay home. So there's often no guaranteed short option in front of your center-backs.
[m43rrgjd38g.3477] - A midfielder who receives facing his own goal, under pressure, can't turn. You go backward or lose it.
[m43rrgjd38g.3581] **The routine**
[m43rrgjd38g.3594] 1. **Make a pivot.** Set one central midfielder to Stay Back While Attacking. He's now always the option in front of your center-backs. If that's not enough, try the 4-3-3 variant with a CDM. FC 26 has no player instructions: make him a Deep-Lying Playmaker with the Build-Up focus.
[m43rrgjd38g.3780] 2. **Use the triangle.** Center-back to full-back to midfielder. Diagonals force the AI to shift, and every shift opens a lane somewhere else.
[m43rrgjd38g.3917] 3. **Up, back, through.** Pass into a midfielder or the striker. He lays it back first-time to a teammate facing forward, who plays the runner.
[m43rrgjd38g.4055] 4. **Draw, then release.** If nobody presses your center-back, walk forward with the ball. When a defender steps out to you, pass to the man he left.
[m43rrgjd38g.4199] 5. **Pass with pace.** Any pass that breaks a line should be a driven ground pass. A soft pass through a lane gives the AI time to step in.
[m43rrgjd38g.4333] 6. **Go over the press.** When they push high, a lofted pass to your full-back or winger skips their first line.
[m43rrgjd38g.4440] 7. **Reset without shame.** Everything covered? Back to the keeper or the far center-back, then switch sides. Even on top difficulty, the AI only presses high for so long.
[m43rrgjd38g.12098] <embed {"id": ".12098", "rev": "11", "h": "bec377cb", "caption": "Build-up pattern \u00b7 3 passes", "ref": "node/29985fb2-128f"}> 
[m43rrgjd38g.12099] The CM can't turn with a defender on his back, so he lays it off first-time to the pivot, who faces forward and plays the winger in.
[m43rrgjd38g.4607] **Drill**
[m43rrgjd38g.4614] - Warm up with the ground-pass and through-pass skill games, five minutes each.
[m43rrgjd38g.4693] - First 10 minutes of every match: no long balls from defense. Every attack starts with your keeper or a center-back.
[m43rrgjd38g.4810] - Count clean progressions: times the ball goes from your third to theirs without being lost. Log the number.
[m43rrgjd38g.4919] ## Gap 2: Creating clear-cut chances
[m43rrgjd38g.4953] Learn three chance patterns like set plays, and repeat each one until the AI stops it. Possession without a final pattern is just circulation.
[m43rrgjd38g.5096] **Why it breaks**
[m43rrgjd38g.5111] - Legendary guards the obvious receiver, exactly as your notes say. Central passes into the box get cut out.
[m43rrgjd38g.5219] - In PC Gamer's testing across dozens of FIFA 23 matches, headers from crosses almost never scored, corners rarely threatened, and goals from beyond about 20 yards were uncommon. Your 4-5 players in the box is tuned for a weapon that doesn't pay much. FC 26 drops the Players in Box setting, and its Squad Battles gameplay adds heading error, so the same holds there.
[m43rrgjd38g.5416] - The through ball is still the strongest final pass, especially down the wings.
[m43rrgjd38g.5496] **The three patterns**
[m43rrgjd38g.5516] 1. **Byline cutback.** Get a winger or overlapping full-back to the byline, then pass back along the ground to the edge of the box. Set one midfielder to Stay On Edge Of Box For Cross so someone always arrives there. FC 26 has no such instruction, but since update 1.2.0 its AI defenders mark the edge of the box less tightly, so look back for the late man.
[m43rrgjd38g.5727] 2. **Overload, then switch.** Three or four short passes on one flank draw the AI across. Then a driven lob to the far winger (LB + RB + X in FC 26), who's now one-on-one. This is your "make them redistribute" note, turned into a weapon.
[m43rrgjd38g.5936] 3. **Pass and go at the edge.** The one-two: the passer sprints past the defender who's watching the ball. Use directed runs to send a teammate exactly into the gap.
[m43rrgjd38g.6096] **Through-ball timing**
[m43rrgjd38g.6117] - Release when your runner is level with the last defender and that defender is facing the ball.
[m43rrgjd38g.6213] - Driven (threaded) through ball for tight gaps on the ground. Lobbed through ball when their line is high. In FC 26, aim at the gap between two defenders: semi-assisted through balls pick the runner in that lane.
[m43rrgjd38g.6320] **Finishing**
[m43rrgjd38g.6331] - Press shoot before the ball arrives for a first-time finish, as your notes say.
[m43rrgjd38g.6412] - In the box: low and placed, or finesse to the far post. Save the power shot for when you have time and space. In FC 26, double-tap shoot for a low driven shot; Timed Finishing is gone.
[m43rrgjd38g.6523] **One feint to add**
[m43rrgjd38g.6540] Learn the fake shot and use it every match. When a defender slides in to block, it buys the half-second for a second shot or a cutback. Former FIFA world champion Gorilla advises drilling a skill move in the practice arena until it's as automatic as a pass.
[m43rrgjd38g.6798] **Drill**
[m43rrgjd38g.6805] - Practice arena: 10 cutback finishes from each side.
[m43rrgjd38g.6858] - In matches: once you're in the final third, look for one of the three patterns. None on? Recycle back and switch sides.
[m43rrgjd38g.6979] ## Gap 3: Balls in behind your back line
[m43rrgjd38g.7017] You concede in behind when your line is high, your center-backs are flat, or you've pulled one forward yourself. One read and a few habits fix most of it.
[m43rrgjd38g.7172] **The read**
[m43rrgjd38g.7182] - The trigger is their midfielder receiving the ball facing your goal, with time. That's when the through ball comes.
[m43rrgjd38g.7299] - Before the pass is played, switch to the center-back on the runner's side and start dropping. Don't wait to react to the pass.
[m43rrgjd38g.7427] **The habits**
[m43rrgjd38g.7439] - Don't control a center-back while the ball is in midfield. Defend with your midfielders first and let the back line hold itself, as Gorilla advises.
[m43rrgjd38g.7589] - When chasing a runner, get goal-side and inside him. Force him wide, away from goal.
[m43rrgjd38g.7675] - Rush your keeper only when the runner's touch is heavy and you'll clearly get there first.
[m43rrgjd38g.7767] **Settings that help**
[m43rrgjd38g.7787] - Set the full-back on the side you attack less to Stay Back While Attacking. You're no longer two-against-two at the back after a turnover. In FC 26, give him the Fullback role with the Defend focus.
[m43rrgjd38g.7927] - Try lowering Depth by about 10. Deeper leaves less space behind, but expect a small effect: in FIFA 22, Gorilla saw almost no difference between depth settings. In FC 26, Depth became Line Height: Balanced defaults to 50, so try 45.
[m43rrgjd38g.8073] **Drill**
[m43rrgjd38g.8080] - Every time their midfielder turns, say "drop" out loud and switch. It feels silly; it builds the reflex.
[m43rrgjd38g.8186] - In your match log, write down how each goal against started.

[m43rrgjd38g.9747] ## Shaking off the rust: first five sessions
[m43rrgjd38g.9789] Rebuild timing before you chase results. Each session is a 10-minute warm-up, then matches with one focus.
[m43rrgjd38g.9897] - **Session 1:** ground-pass skill game, then one World Class match and one Legendary match. Focus: apply the tactics changes and run the build-up routine only.
[m43rrgjd38g.10053] - **Session 2:** through-pass skill game, then two Legendary matches. Focus: up, back, through, plus the reset switch.
[m43rrgjd38g.10167] - **Session 3:** cutback finishes in the practice arena, then two Legendary matches. Focus: the three chance patterns.
[m43rrgjd38g.10281] - **Session 4:** ground-pass skill game, then two Legendary matches. Focus: the through-ball read. Say "drop," and never control a center-back in midfield.
[m43rrgjd38g.10432] - **Session 5:** warm-up of your choice, then two or three Legendary matches. Focus: everything. Review the log and change one setting.
[m43rrgjd38g.10563] The World Class match in session 1 is a timing warm-up, not a step down. Go straight back to Legendary after it.
[m43rrgjd38g.15317] On FC 26, swap the skill games for five minutes in the Practice Arena and one round of the Scan trainer in the Replay Room.
