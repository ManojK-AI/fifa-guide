    The approach

    Look before the ball gets to you

    A study of Premier League players found that the more a player looked around before getting the ball, the more likely his pass was to arrive. FIFA works the same way, just faster.

    1. Danger. Where is the nearest black shirt?
    2. Free man. Which white shirt has nobody within two yards?
    3. Space. Is their line high? Is your runner level with it?

    Do the three looks while the pass is still rolling to you: the far side first, then the radar. Decide before your first touch.

    Source: Jordet and others (2020), scanning in Premier League players

    When it attacks you

    Nine moves to read and stop.

    1Through ball behind your line

    You’ll seeTheir midfielder facing forward, a striker level with your last defender, and one of your CBs stepping out.

    Do thisHold your line. Let a midfielder press. Switch (LB) to the CB nearest the runner and stay goal-side.

    WhyEA made the CPU play more direct (Title Update 2) and then made its through balls more effective (Title Update 4).

    fut.gg, Title Update 2 · fut.gg, Title Update 4 · Red Bull, how to defend

    2Winger down the touchline, then a cross

    You’ll seeA quick winger with grass ahead of him on the touchline, or already at your byline.

    Do thisFull-back goal-side and jockey (LT): show him outside. CBs stay in the box. Switch to the near-post CB before the cross.

    WhyEA says the CPU uses the touchlines more and is more willing to cross. Early Crossers cross early.

    EA Pitch Notes · Charlie Intel, player traits · Gfinity, how to defend

    3Dribbler on a long run

    You’ll seeA quick or tricky player carrying it from deep and cutting past people.

    Do thisJockey (LT) a yard off. Tackle (B) when he slows or the ball comes loose. No slide tackles in or near your box.

    WhySpeed and Technical Dribblers go on long runs. On Legendary and Ultimate some CPU players get +10 on every stat with the ball.

    Charlie Intel, player traits · EA Pitch Notes · Gfinity, how to defend

    4First-time shot from the edge

    You’ll seeA midfielder arriving late at the edge of your box while the ball is out wide.

    Do thisKeep one midfielder on the edge. Close the runner while the pass is travelling and get in the line of the shot.

    WhyFirst Time Shots is a CPU-only trait: those players shoot the moment the ball arrives. Long Shot Takers shoot from distance.

    EA Pitch Notes · Charlie Intel, player traits

    5Striker through on your keeper

    You’ll seeA one-on-one.

    Do thisBring your keeper halfway with Y, then let him set. Don’t charge all the way.

    WhyCPU players with Chip Shot or Round the Keeper punish a keeper who comes too far. Guides bring the keeper halfway to make the striker decide.

    Charlie Intel, player traits · EA Pitch Notes · Sportskeeda, defending tips · FIFPlay, defending tips

    6Their playmaker

    You’ll seeThe same player keeps getting the ball and moves it on fast.

    Do thisStand in the lane to him and press him the moment he receives.

    WhyThe CPU passes to Playmakers more often, and they decide faster once they have it.

    Charlie Intel, player traits

    7Long switch to the far side

    You’ll seeYour whole team shifted to the ball and their far winger alone.

    Do thisKeep your far full-back close enough to step out. Don’t all chase the ball.

    WhyLong Passers are more likely to try long passes, and the CPU uses the touchlines more.

    Charlie Intel, player traits · EA Pitch Notes

    8Late push when it’s losing

    You’ll seeThey’re behind late: attacking subs, a more attacking shape, full-backs high.

    Do thisKeep the ball at the back. When you win it, counter at once into the space behind.

    WhyEA: the CPU changes its formation and makes subs during the match to chase a result.

    EA Pitch Notes · Twinfinite, Squad Battles tips (FIFA 22)

    9Corners

    You’ll seeTheir tallest players moving into attacking spots.

    Do thisControl a CB and pick up their tallest man. Clear it wide.

    WhyEA moved taller players into attacking spots at corners in Title Update 7.

    When it defends

    Nine habits to break.

    1Marks and reacts at 100%

    You’ll seePasses into feet getting cut out.

    Do thisPass into space and into runs. Move the ball quickly.

    WhyOn Ultimate its reactions and marking go from 90% to 100%. Its defending stays at 90%.

    FIFPlay, difficulty levels

    2Presses, then is slow to get back

    You’ll seeA midfielder charging at your man, then stuck behind the ball once you’re past.

    Do thisTwo quick passes out of the press, then drive forward.

    WhyEA: CPU players who press and miss take longer to track back (Title Update 6).

    fut.gg, Title Update 6

    3Only blocks up close

    You’ll seeA defender within a yard of your shooter.

    Do thisFake shot (B then A), then shoot with space. Fakes, feints and stepovers fool it even on high levels.

    WhyEA: CPU blocks reach about 1 m. Guides say fake shots still work against the AI.

    EA Pitch Notes · FIFA Infinity, Squad Battles tips · Twinfinite, Squad Battles tips (FIFA 22)

    4Its keeper needs to see the ball

    You’ll seeA defender a few yards off, between you and the keeper.

    Do thisShoot now, low, past him.

    WhyEA: keepers react slower when players block their view.

    EA Pitch Notes

    5Closes a sprinting carrier fast

    You’ll seeTwo of them stepping up as your player sprints.

    Do thisDon’t sprint into traffic. Pass and go (LB + A), then take the return.

    WhyIf you sprint without space in front of you, the AI closes the gap and takes the ball.

    FIFA Infinity, Squad Battles tips

    6Sits deep and compact

    You’ll seeEveryone behind the ball and the middle shut.

    Do thisGo round it: through balls out wide, then low cutbacks. Few long shots go in.

    WhyPC Gamer: AI teams play compact with a deep block, and the through ball is king.

    PC Gamer

    7Holds a high line

    You’ll seeTheir back four near halfway and your striker level with them.

    Do thisLobbed (LB + Y) or driven lobbed (LB + RB + Y) through ball over the top.

    WhyEA: high-depth teams hold a high line and push up when you retreat.

    EA Pitch Notes · Push Square

    8Keeper rushes in a one-on-one

    You’ll seeTheir keeper sprinting at your striker.

    Do thisChip it (LB + B) just before he reaches you.

    WhyTitle Update 2 made chip shots more accurate.

    Sportskeeda, scoring tips · fut.gg, Title Update 2

    9Chases the game when losing

    You’ll seeThey push players forward late.

    Do thisSoak it up, then counter fast into the space.

    WhyEA: the CPU changes formation and subs to chase a game.

    Your match script

    Six steps, every game

    1. Kick-off. Pass back to the edge of the circle, then a driven lob over their line to your striker (LB + RB + Y). RealSport101
    2. On the ball. Look first, then pass into space. Through balls and cutbacks, not long shots or high crosses. PC Gamer
    3. In the box. Defender within a yard? Fake shot. Keeper rushing out? Chip it (LB + B). Sportskeeda
    4. Defending. Hold your line and press with midfielders, not CBs. Jockey, never dive in. Push Square
    5. You win it. Go forward fast. Their pressers are slow to get back. fut.gg
    6. Leading late. Pass it around your back four and keeper. When they push everyone up to chase it, counter into the space. PC Gamer · EA Pitch Notes

    Your squad

    Players that fit this plan

    • Striker: Lengthy. A Lengthy striker gets to over-the-top balls quicker than the other types. fut.gg
    • Wingers and full-backs: Explosive or Controlled. Since Title Update 4 they are quicker with the ball at their feet. fut.gg
    • Quick defenders. They recover from over-the-top through balls, the CPU’s favourite pass. Sportskeeda · fut.gg
    • Quick full-backs, tall CBs. Quick full-backs block the crosses from rapid wingers; tall CBs head them away. Gfinity
    • Pace up front. Quick runners going in behind were a big part of beating Ultimate in Squad Battles. Gfinity (written for FIFA 21)

    Straight talk

    What this can and can’t do

    No guide promises you’ll beat Ultimate every game. It is the hardest level, and it only exists in Ultimate Team. What the sources agree on: play into space, go fast once you win the ball, and defend with patience.

    This page trains your reads and shows the moves. The rest is reps: win most of your Squad Battles on Legendary, then step up to Ultimate.
