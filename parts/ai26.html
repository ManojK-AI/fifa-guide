Squad Battles AI in FC 26

GameplayCompetitive, always. EA locks it in Ultimate Team.
TirednessEA says CPU-controlled players in Squad Battles aren’t affected by fatigue.
Since update 1.4.0The Squad Battles AI attacks more. AI defenders on both sides react slower.
Ultimate vs LegendaryEA hasn’t published the numbers for FC 26.

When it attacks you

Nine moves to read and stop.

1Goes forward fast

You’ll seeTheir midfielder looking forward the moment he gets it, instead of passing it sideways.

Do thisGet your shape first. Press with a midfielder, keep your CBs home, and press LB to switch to the CB nearest their runner.

WhySince update 1.4.0 (January 2026) EA says the Squad Battles AI takes “a more proactive and attacking approach” and won’t keep the ball as much as before.

EA, update 1.4.0

2Through ball between your CBs

You’ll seeTheir striker level with your last defender and a gap opening between your two CBs.

Do thisDon’t step out with a CB. Jockey (LT) goal-side of the runner and let a midfielder press the passer.

WhySince update 1.1.3 the AI looks for passes more often and decides quicker. A player reports pinpoint through balls between two defenders.

EA, update 1.1.3 notes · EA forums, player report

3Sharp dribbler

You’ll seeA dribbler cutting back and changing direction instead of running in a straight line.

Do thisJockey (LT) a yard off, on his inside. Never tackle from behind. Tackle (B) when he slows or turns his back.

WhyUpdate 1.1.3 made the AI dribble with sharper turns and less time on the ball. EA also made tackles from behind less effective, and jockey quicker (update 1.1.0).

EA, update 1.1.3 notes · EA, FC 26 Gameplay Deep Dive · EA, update 1.1.0

4Late shot from the edge of your box

You’ll seeThe ball goes wide near your box and their midfielder arrives late at the edge.

Do thisKeep one midfielder on the edge. Control him and close the runner while the pass travels.

WhyEA says AI players are less timid about shooting and power up shots like a person. Since update 1.2.0 AI defenders, yours included, mark the edge of the box a little less tightly.

EA, Closed Beta feedback update · EA, FC 26 Gameplay Deep Dive · EA, update 1.2.0 notes

5Crosses into your box

You’ll seeA winger with grass ahead of him on the touchline, or already at your byline.

Do thisFull-back goal-side and jockey him outside. CBs stay in the box. Switch to the near-post CB before the cross.

WhyPull a CB out to the winger and your box is short. Update 1.3.0 lowered crosses, so they come in flatter.

EA, update 1.3.0 notes

6Quick passing through your lines

You’ll seeShort, fast passes into feet between your midfield and defence.

Do thisStand in the passing lane and let the man come to you. Don’t chase the ball.

WhyUpdate 1.5.0 made ground passes quicker and more accurate, most of all for good passers, the AI’s included.

EA, update 1.5.0

7Strong man shielding the ball

You’ll seeA big forward with his back to you, holding you off.

Do thisDon’t barge him from behind. Stay goal-side, bring a second man with hold RB, and win it when he turns.

WhyUpdate 1.4.0 gave Strength and Aggression more say in shielding, and EA cut the success of tackles from behind.

EA, update 1.4.0 · EA, FC 26 Gameplay Deep Dive

8Striker through on your keeper

You’ll seeTheir striker clean through, your keeper on his line.

Do thisHold Y briefly to bring the keeper halfway, then let him set. Don’t charge all the way.

WhyUpdate 1.5.0 made chip shots more accurate, more so for strikers with the Chip Shot PlayStyle. The halfway idea itself is a FIFA 23 tip.

EA, update 1.5.0 · Sportskeeda (FIFA 23 tip)

9Corners

You’ll seeTheir strongest players lining up in your box.

Do thisPut your strongest headers on the defending-corner jobs FC 26 added: Near Post, Back Post and Threat Marker.

WhyFC 26 added those corner roles, and EA says stronger players now win the battle for a high ball.

EA, FC 26 Gameplay Deep Dive

When it defends

Nine habits to break.

1Slow to react in transition

You’ll seeYou beat their first press and their defenders are slow to react.

Do thisGo now. Drive forward or play the runner before they reset.

WhyUpdate 1.4.0: AI defenders react slower when marking the man on the ball, which leaves space while they reset.

EA, update 1.4.0 notes

2Looser line, fewer automatic blocks

You’ll seeGaps in their back line and a defender a few yards off your striker.

Do thisShoot early with a low driven shot (double-tap B), or slide the runner into the gap.

WhySquad Battles uses Competitive gameplay. EA lists “less AI defending, resulting in looser defensive lines” and “fewer auto tackles and auto blocks”.

EA, FC 26 Gameplay Deep Dive

3Gaps between two defenders

You’ll seeA runner and a lane between two of their defenders.

Do thisAim the through ball (Y) at the gap, not at the player. Through Pass Assistance on Semi.

WhyFC 26 semi-assisted through balls use passing lanes: aim at the gap between two opponents and the game picks the runner in it.

EA, FC 26 Gameplay Deep Dive · EA, update 1.1.0

4Edge of its box left open

You’ll seeTheir defenders dropping toward goal while your midfielder arrives late.

Do thisGet to the byline, then cut it back to the edge of the box.

WhySince update 1.2.0 AI defenders mark the edge of their box a little less tightly.

EA, update 1.2.0 notes

5Two men close you down

You’ll seeTwo of them stepping up to your man on the ball.

Do thisPass and move. First-time passes, then run into the space they left.

WhyRed Bull’s FC 26 meta guide rates quick, first-time passing as one of the strongest attacks, and calls first-time passes “extremely powerful”.

Red Bull, FC 26 meta guide

6Blocks up close

You’ll seeA defender within a yard when your striker wants to shoot.

Do thisFake shot first (B then A, with a direction), then shoot with space.

WhyClose up he can get a foot to it. Update 1.5.0 made the Fake Shot 5% faster.

EA, update 1.5.0

7Keeper in a one-on-one

You’ll seeYour striker through, their keeper deciding whether to come.

Do thisIf he rushes out, chip it (LB + B). If he stays home, low driven (double-tap B) to a corner.

WhyEA says FC 26 keepers make “more reasonable” one-on-one decisions, and update 1.5.0 made chips more accurate.

EA, FC 26 Gameplay Deep Dive · EA, update 1.5.0

8Finesse from the edge, not from range

You’ll seeSpace at the edge of the box after a cut inside.

Do thisCut inside, then finesse to the far post (RB + B). From long range, use a power shot (LB + RB + B).

WhyAt launch EA cut the top spin on finesse shots from outside the box. Update 1.5.0 made power shots better from range. Players still rate the stronger Finesse Shot+ PlayStyle in Squad Battles.

EA, Launch update · EA, update 1.5.0 · EA forums, player tip

9Leaves space when it attacks

You’ll seeTheir full-backs and midfielders forward when you win the ball.

Do thisWin it, then go straight into the space behind them.

WhyUpdate 1.4.0 made the Squad Battles AI more attacking. A player on EA’s forums says it “seems to really struggle with counter attacks”.

EA, update 1.4.0 · EA forums, player tip
