Defending

Defend by hand

  • Choose your defending mode on purpose. Tactical: hold A contains and the game picks the tackle. Advanced, the default: hold A is a physical tackle, RB + B a hard tackle, RB + X a hard slide. Stay on Tactical until your tackles are timed, then try Advanced for the extra tools. EAGuideCoach’s call EA settings guide · FIFPlay controls · FIFPlay defending
  • Sprint jockey LT+RT keeps you goal-side of a runner. Update 1.1.0 made jockey and sprint jockey faster and more responsive. GuideEA FIFPlay defending · EA 1.1.0
  • Tackle when the ball leaves his foot or when he turns his back to you. Never from behind: EA made tackles from behind less effective. EACoach’s call EA Deep Dive
  • Pick defenders by Stand Tackle. 85 or more gets every tackle animation, 71 to 84 most of them, under 71 only the basic ones. EA EA Deep Dive
  • Shoulder only a weaker man. Since update 1.4.0 a big Strength gap makes the weaker player stumble. Bruiser was toned down in May 2026. EA EA 1.4.0 · EA May 2026 notes (MP1st)
  • Put your interceptors in the lanes. Since May 2026 players with 90+ Interceptions get the Intercept PlayStyle’s animations. EA EA May 2026 notes (MP1st)
  • Second man in bursts. Hold RB for about a second, then let go before you tackle so he drops back into his lane. Since May 2026 AI teammates stop doubling up while you mark the man yourself, so get tight first. EACoach’s call EA May 2026 notes (MP1st)
  • Pressing calls. Partial team press: double-tap RB and hold. Team Press: D-pad Up, then Left, in their half only since May 2026. Offside trap: Up, then Up, only when your line is set and their passer is under pressure. GuideEACoach’s call Operation Sports pressing · EA May 2026 notes (MP1st)
  • Leave your keeper alone. Update 1.3.0 greatly cut manual keeper movement and removed Rush to Center in open play. Hold Y only to come halfway. EA EA 1.3.0
  • Switch to the man in the lane, not the nearest one: flick the right stick. Keep the Next Player Switch Indicator on. EACoach’s call EA settings guide

Movement and dribbling

Move like a forward

  • Controlled sprint: hold RB while dribbling to carry it fast and close. Since update 1.1.0 you can trigger directed runs during it. GuideEA FIFPlay controls · EA 1.1.0 · EA 1.1.0 notes
  • Directed run: tap LB, then flick the right stick where you want him to go. Send one as a decoy and pass into the gap it leaves. GuideCoach’s call FIFPlay controls
  • Player lock: press both sticks in, LS+RS, to take a player off the ball and make his run yourself; press both again to let go. Some players say it doesn’t work in Ultimate Team, so try it in Kick-off first. Unconfirmed Operation Sports player lock
  • First touch: hold LT as the pass arrives for a cleaner touch, at the cost of speed. EA EA Deep Dive
  • Loose balls: press RT to make your man attack the ball. EA EA Deep Dive
  • Shield: hold LT. Since update 1.4.0 Strength and Aggression decide more of it, and Enforcer got a boost in May 2026. EA EA 1.4.0 · EA May 2026 notes (MP1st)
  • Left-stick dribbling works better again in FC 26, best with Quick Step players. Guide Red Bull meta guide

  • The line ranges. Deep 1–30 (default 25), Balanced 31–60 (50), High 61–90 (70), Aggressive 91–100 (95, presses heavy touches, drains stamina). A pro puts the ceiling at 60: “the highest your defensive line can be without taking unnecessary risks”. EAGuide EA FC IQ (FC 25) · Red Bull tactics
  • Tactical Focus on D-pad Right shifts both Build-Up and Defensive Approach a step: Right, then Right for attacking; Right, then Left for defending. EA described it this way for FC 25. EAGuide EA FC IQ (FC 25) · FIFPlay controls

  • Role, Role+ and Role++. EA says familiarity can make 10% to 40% of the difference (FC 25), and FC 26 brought Role and Role+ “closer to Role++” and cut the out-of-position penalty. Chase ++ in the pivot and striker roles, but don’t drop a better player for it. EACoach’s call EA FC IQ (FC 25) · EA Deep Dive
  • New FC 26 roles. Box Crasher: a CDM who makes trailing runs into the box, made for Chase. Ball-Playing Keeper: an option in build-up who stays on his line in defence. EACoach’s call EA Deep Dive
  • A pro’s offline view: “make the pitch as big as possible”, with four at the back and three central midfielders or a 4-2-3-1, and wide players on the Winger role. 4-2-3-1 Wide is the alternative Control plan to test. Guide Red Bull tactics

Set pieces

Dead balls, live chances

  • Corner jobs. FC 26 added attacking roles Top of Box and Defensive Cover, and defending ones Threat Marker, Post Watcher, Near Post and Back Post. Put a good finisher on Top of Box for the ball that comes back out. EACoach’s call EA Deep Dive
  • Go short more often. In Competitive gameplay, which Squad Battles uses, fewer attackers go up for corners by default and headers miss more. A short corner and a cutback beats a 50/50 header. Crossing anyway? Overload Set Pieces (Up, then Right) adds bodies, per one guide. EAGuideCoach’s call EA Deep Dive · FIFAUTeam quick tactics
  • Throw-ins. Press RB to call your chosen throw-in taker. Long throws need the Long Throw PlayStyle. EA EA Deep Dive
  • Free kicks. EA re-tuned free-kick flight when it removed Timed Finishing. Guides disagree on power, from about 1.5 to 3.5 bars, so find your taker’s number in the Practice Arena, which lets you change the kick-taker in FC 26. Use a Dead Ball PlayStyle player. EAUnconfirmed EA Deep Dive · RealSport101 · Team Gullit · FIFPlay Practice Arena
  • Penalties. No FC 26 method could be confirmed. Practise them in the Practice Arena before you trust one.

Practice

Train like it matters

Pros don’t just play more matches. They drill one thing, measure it, then test it in a match. Here is a week that works that way. Coach’s call

  1. Practice Arena, 15 minutes, twice a week. Ten first-time low drivens from cutbacks, ten finesse shots from the edge, ten chips past a keeper coming out. Move up a station at 8 out of 10.
  2. One skill move a week: 50 reps in the arena, then use it in matches only in its moment from the table above.
  3. Scan trainer at Ultimate speed (1.4 seconds) until you score 8 out of 10 twice in a row.
  4. After every match, open Match Facts. One guide says it shows Expected Goals and Ball Recovery Time. Log your xG for and against, and how each goal against started.
  5. Change one thing at a time: a tactic setting or a habit, every two or three matches, then check the log.
