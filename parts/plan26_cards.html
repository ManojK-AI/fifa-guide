Your match script

Six steps, every game

  1. Kick-off. FC 26 defenders sit compact right after kick-off. Pass back, keep it, and go over the top (LB + Y) only if their line is still high. EA update 1.0.3 notes
  2. On the ball. Look first. Quick, first-time passes. Aim through balls at the gap between two defenders. Red Bull meta guide · EA Deep Dive
  3. In the box. Cut it back to the edge. Defender within a yard? Fake shot. Keeper rushing out? Chip it (LB + B). Otherwise a low driven shot: double-tap B. EA update 1.2.0 notes · EA update 1.5.0
  4. Defending. Hold your line and press with midfielders, not CBs. Jockey (LT), and never tackle from behind. EA Deep Dive · EA update 1.1.0
  5. You win it. Go forward fast. Since update 1.4.0 AI defenders react slower when marking the man on the ball. EA update 1.4.0 notes
  6. Leading late. Keep the ball. If they push up, win it and counter: a player on EA’s forums says the AI “seems to really struggle with counter attacks”. EA forums (player tip)

Your squad

Players that fit this plan

  • Striker: tall and strong. Since update 1.4.0 more tall players get Lengthy acceleration: a man needs 185 cm or more, Strength 65+, Strength at least 4 above Agility, and Acceleration 40+. Strength also wins shoulder challenges and battles for high balls. EA update 1.4.0 · EA Deep Dive
  • Wingers and full-backs: Explosive. Quick off the mark over short bursts, for beating a man and for getting back. EA Deep Dive · FIFAUTeam AcceleRATE
  • CBs: strong and good in the air. Strength wins the battle for high balls at corners, and the new Aerial Fortress PlayStyle wins defensive headers. EA Deep Dive
  • PlayStyles worth having. Finesse Shot and Low Driven Shot up front, Incisive Pass in midfield, Jockey and Intercept at the back. FIFPlay PlayStyles
  • Pace up front. Fast counters are one of FC 26’s strongest attacks, and players beating Squad Battles lean on fast players. Red Bull meta guide · EA forums (player tip)

Straight talk

What this can and can’t do

No guide promises you’ll beat Ultimate every game. EA retuned the Squad Battles AI in November 2025 (update 1.1.3) and January 2026 (update 1.4.0), and hasn’t published Legendary vs Ultimate numbers for FC 26.

What every source agrees on: play into space, go fast once you win it, defend with patience. Set Defending to Tactical so your buttons match these clips. Then reps: win most Squad Battles on Legendary before you step up to Ultimate.
